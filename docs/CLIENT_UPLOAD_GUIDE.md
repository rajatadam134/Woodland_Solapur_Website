# Woodland Solapur - Client Product Upload Guide

## How to Add New Product Photos

1. **Access Admin Portal:**
   * Open your browser and go to your website link ending with `/admin.html` (e.g. `https://rajatadam134.github.io/Woodland_Solapur_Website/admin.html`).
   * Enter passcode: `Woodlandsolapur@123` and click **Access Portal**.

2. **Select Category & Product Name:**
   * (Optional) Type the **Product Name** (e.g., *Windsor Teak Bed*).
   * Pick **Category** from dropdown (*Living Room*, *Bedroom*, *Office*, *Dining*), or choose **+ Add Custom Category** to type a new category (e.g., *Patio*).

3. **Background Cleaning & Studio Backdrop:**
   * Keep **Apply AI Background Removal** checked. The browser will automatically remove photo background and place your product on a clean studio grey backdrop (`#F5F5F7`) with soft shadows.

4. **Upload Photos:**
   * Drag & drop your photos into the upload box (or click box to select files).
   * Supports 1 photo, 20 photos, or up to 200+ photos in bulk.
   * Heavy camera photos are auto-compressed to light WebP format.

5. **View Live Products:**
   * Open `products.html` on your website. Your new products and category filter pills will appear automatically with instant **Inquire on WhatsApp** buttons!
